# 8-Week Transformation Challenge, Plan & Rulebook

## 1. Overview

An 8-week fitness/weight-loss challenge for your gym community, with a **$2,000 prize pool** split among the top 3 finishers.

**Suggested prize split:**
| Place | % of Pool | Amount |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| 1st | 50% | $1,000 |
| 2nd | 30% | $600 |
| 3rd | 20% | $400 |

This keeps 2nd and 3rd meaningfully motivated instead of an all-or-nothing race.

---

## 2. How Winners Are Determined

**Use % of body weight lost, not raw pounds.** This is the industry-standard method (used by shows like "Biggest Loser," corporate wellness challenges, etc.) because it levels the playing field between people of different starting sizes.

Formula: `(Starting Weight − Final Weight) / Starting Weight × 100`

**Optional alternative/hybrid scoring** if you want to reward more than the scale:
- 70% weight-loss %, 30% "engagement points" (workout attendance, weekly check-ins, photo/measurement submissions)
- This discourages crash-dieting purely to win and rewards consistency.

**Tie-breaker:** total attendance/check-ins during the 8 weeks.

---

## 3. Eligibility & Entry

- Open to active gym members only (verify membership).
- **Minimum age:** 18+ (or 16+ with parental consent, your call).
- **Health screening:** Require participants to confirm no medical conditions that make rapid weight loss unsafe. Consider a simple liability waiver + "cleared by physician if applicable" checkbox, especially since money is involved and it can tempt unsafe behavior.
- **Entry fee (optional):** Many gyms charge $20–50/person to enter, which helps offset the prize pool and filters for serious participants. Not required, but worth considering if you want this to be self-funding going forward.
- **Cap participation** if needed (e.g., first 40 sign-ups) so weigh-ins stay manageable.

---

## 4. Timeline

| Week | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| Week 0 | Sign-ups close, waivers signed, **official Weigh-In & Measurement Day** (photos, weight, waist/hip measurements) |
| Week 1–7 | Weekly check-ins (weigh-in optional, but recommended weekly for accountability) |
| Week 4 | **Midpoint Weigh-In**, great for a leaderboard update and re-motivation push |
| Week 8 | **Final Weigh-In & Measurement Day**, winners calculated, prize ceremony |

**Weigh-in protocol (important for fairness):**
- Same scale, same time of day (morning, fasted, minimal clothing), same person administering it each time.
- Log results privately, don't post individual weights publicly, only % progress or rank, to protect privacy and reduce shame-based pressure.

---

## 5. Rules to Prevent Unsafe Behavior

Because cash is on the line, some people will be tempted to cut corners. Build in guardrails:

- **Minimum weekly weight-loss cap**, e.g., disqualify anyone losing more than ~2–3% body weight in a single week without a documented medical reason (a common industry safeguard against dehydration tricks, extreme fasting, etc.)
- No participation from anyone who is already underweight (BMI < 18.5) or has a history of disordered eating, reframe eligibility toward general fitness/body recomposition rather than a pure "lose the most" contest if this is a concern in your community.
- Require gym staff/trainer sign-off before final winner is confirmed.

---

## 6. Weekly Engagement Ideas (Keep People Motivated for 8 Weeks)

Momentum drops around week 3–5, plan for it:

- **Weekly themes:** Strength Week, Cardio Blast Week, Nutrition Focus Week, Mobility/Recovery Week
- **Mini-challenges with small non-cash prizes** (free protein shake, merch, guest pass) to keep engagement between weigh-ins, e.g., "most classes attended this week"
- **Group accountability:** private Facebook group, gym leaderboard on the wall, or a simple shared spreadsheet
- **Buddy system:** pair participants for weekly check-ins
- **Optional nutrition support:** bring in a coach for a group Q&A rather than prescribing individual diets. This keeps you out of giving medical/nutrition advice you're not licensed for
