# 8-Week Challenge, Rules, Regulations & Accountability System

## 1. Program Format & Pricing

This challenge is **community-based and remote (non-face-to-face) by default**, with an optional in-person upgrade.

| Tier | Price | Format |
|------|-------|--------|
| **Standard (Community)** | **$35/week** ($280 total for 8 weeks) | Fully remote, online check-ins, community group, self-reported tracking |
| **F2F Upgrade** | **$99/week** ($792 total for 8 weeks) | In-person coaching sessions in addition to everything in Standard |

**Payment structure recommendation:**
- Charge the full 8-week amount up front ($280 or $792) rather than weekly billing. This reduces admin overhead and drop-off risk, and is standard for challenge-style programs.
- Alternative: 2 payments (50% at sign-up, 50% at week 4) if you want to lower the barrier to entry.
- **Refund policy** (recommend stating clearly at sign-up): e.g., "No refunds after Week 2 weigh-in, as prize eligibility and program costs are already committed." Adjust to your comfort level.

**What's included in Standard ($35/wk):**
- Access to private community group (Facebook/Discord/Telegram, pick one)
- Weekly check-in form
- Weekly accountability tasks & leaderboard
- Prize pool eligibility

**What's included in F2F Upgrade ($99/wk):**
- Everything in Standard, plus
- 1x weekly in-person session (coaching, form checks, guided workout, define exactly what this includes)
- Priority support/adjustments

## 2. Eligibility & Registration

- Open to the community (not restricted to existing gym members, since this is remote-first).
- Must be **18+** or 16+ with signed parental/guardian consent.
- Must complete and sign the **Waiver & Health Disclosure** before Week 0 weigh-in (template below).
- Must submit **Week 0 baseline weigh-in** (self-reported, with verification method, see Section 4) to be eligible for prizes.
- Payment must be received in full (or per your payment plan) before Week 0 to be officially enrolled.

---

## 3. Prize Structure & Eligibility

**$2,000 total pool, split:**
- 1st place: $1,000 (50%)
- 2nd place: $600 (30%)
- 3rd place: $400 (20%)

**Scored by:** % of body weight lost from Week 0 baseline to Week 8 final.

**To be eligible for prize money, a participant must:**
- Submit Week 0 and Week 8 official weigh-ins (see verification method below)
- Complete a minimum of **6 out of 8 weekly accountability check-ins** (see Section 5)
- Not be disqualified under Section 6

*(This prevents someone from disappearing for 7 weeks and reappearing just to win money off one good final weigh-in.)*

---

## 4. Weigh-In & Verification Protocol (Remote)

Since this is non-F2F, self-reported data needs a verification layer to keep it fair:

- **Weigh-in submission:** Participant submits a timestamped photo of themselves on the scale (numbers visible) each official weigh-in week (Week 0, Week 4, Week 8), via the check-in form or group.
- **Consistency rule:** Same scale, same time of day (recommend: morning, fasted, minimal clothing), stated at sign-up.
- **Random spot-checks:** Reserve the right to request a live video weigh-in for top-ranking participants before finalizing winners. This is standard practice for remote challenges to prevent gaming the system.
- **Privacy:** Weigh-in photos/numbers are for admin verification only, never posted publicly. Leaderboard displays % progress and rank only, not raw weight.

---

## 5. Weekly Accountability Tasks

Each week, participants submit via the check-in form:

| Task | Required? | Points |
|------|-----------|--------|
| Weekly check-in form (weight trend, energy, adherence self-rating) | Required | 2 pts |
| Workout log (sessions completed that week) | Required | 2 pts |
| Progress photo (private, admin-only) | Optional | 1 pt |
| Community engagement (1 post/comment in group) | Optional | 1 pt |
| Nutrition/water/sleep log | Optional | 1 pt |

**Weekly deadline:** Recommend Sunday night, so you can post an updated leaderboard Monday morning to kick off the week.

**Point totals are the tie-breaker** if two participants finish with identical % weight loss, and also serve as the "6 of 8 weeks" completion tracker for prize eligibility.

---

## 6. Rules of Conduct & Disqualification

Participant is **disqualified from prize eligibility** (may still remain in the community/program) if they:

- Miss more than 2 weekly check-ins without prior notice
- Fail to submit Week 0 or Week 8 official weigh-in
- Are found to have falsified weigh-in data or photos
- Lose more than ~3% of body weight in a single week without a documented medical explanation (safety flag, reach out to check in, don't just penalize)
- Engage in harassment, body-shaming, or negativity toward other participants in the community space

**Health & safety note:** This is a wellness challenge, not a race to extremes. Make clear in your messaging that rapid, unsafe weight loss disqualifies rather than wins, this protects you legally and protects participants.

## 7. Communication & Community Space
