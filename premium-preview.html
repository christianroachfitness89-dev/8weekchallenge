The Experience

Built for people who are serious about change

Every week has a rhythm. You know exactly what to do, when to check in, and how you are tracking. The structure removes decision fatigue so you can focus on execution.

Monday - Leaderboard drops

See exactly where you stand against your challenge. Rankings are based on % body weight lost, so every starting point is fair.

Wednesday - Mid-week check-in

Share a workout, a win, or a struggle in the private community feed. Momentum is built in the middle of the week.

Sunday - Weigh-in deadline

Submit your weekly form before the new week starts. Same scale, same time of day, in KG.

Week 0, 4, 8 - Official weigh-ins

Photo-verified weigh-ins keep the competition honest and protect the integrity of the prize pool.
